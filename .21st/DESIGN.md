# DaSHe Books design context

The site is a premium, cover-led bookstore for readers of Kehinde Akinlolu OJO. Its visual language is kinetic editorial: large literary typography, high-contrast ink and acid colour, asymmetrical layouts, genuine cover artwork, and motion used to clarify browsing and selection.

The interface is mobile-first. Interactive controls remain at least 44px, all motion has a reduced-motion fallback, cover images are optimized WebP assets, and the catalogue supports keyboard use, search, filtering and accessible detail dialogs.
